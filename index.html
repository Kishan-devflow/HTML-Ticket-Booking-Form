GOOD TO KNOW

Booking guidelines

A few quick notes to make this demo experience clear and easy.

Start a demo booking ↗
01 Are these official IPL fixtures?

No. Matchups are examples for the demo only. Dates, times and venues are not official and have not been confirmed.

02 Does this website sell or reserve tickets?

No. There is no ticketing backend or payment processing. Saving a preview only stores demo details in this browser.

03 Where are my demo bookings saved?

They are saved in this browser's local storage on this device. They are not sent to a server and may be cleared with browser data.

04 What should I check before a real match?

Use official IPL and team channels for confirmed schedules, stadium information, ticket availability and entry requirements.
